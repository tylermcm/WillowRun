New patients

Whether it’s been six months or six years, we’re glad you’re here.

No lectures, no judgment. Just a friendly team and a plan that makes sense for you. Getting started takes three easy steps.

  1. Step 1

    Give us a call

    Ring (720) 872-2750 and we’ll find a time that works for you and your family.

  2. Step 2

    Fill out your forms online

    Complete everything from your phone or computer before your visit. It takes about 10 minutes.

  3. Step 3

    Bring your insurance card

    We’ll check your coverage and network status and explain what to expect.

Do your paperwork online

Health history, financial policy, privacy notice and records release, one friendly step at a time. Nothing is saved on our website, and you can sign with your finger.
