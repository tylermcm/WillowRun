Meet your dentist

Dr. Lonnie L. McMurtrey, D.D.S.

His goal is to help you get and keep a healthy smile for life, in an office where the patient always comes first and your comfort comes before everything else.

Dr. McMurtrey is a family dentist who has practiced in Colorado since 1996 and right here in Westminster since January 2002. Patients tell us he’s the kind of dentist who comes out to the waiting room to say hello and takes the time each person needs.

At home, he and his wife Christa, married since 1994, have raised three children. The family loves camping, riding motorcycles and ATVs, and traveling.
